The problem

Accountants look backward. You need to look forward.

Traditional reports show what happened last month — not whether liquidity will run tight six weeks from now.

Without LiquiBot

Delayed historical numbers, no early warning before a shortfall, raw material and energy price swings hit unprepared, decisions made by gut feel.

With LiquiBot

Continuous liquidity forecasting, explainable AI recommendations, price swings priced in early, concrete actions instead of just a dashboard.

Why LiquiBot

Three things we do differently

No generic template — a system that actually understands your business.

01

Tailored forecasting

Learns your company's individual payment patterns, seasonality, and cost structure — instead of applying a generic template to everyone.

02

Explainable AI

Shows why a forecast looks the way it does — traced back to concrete drivers using SHAP. No black box.

03

From monitoring to action

Concrete recommendations, not just numbers: e.g. when to adjust payment terms to close a projected liquidity gap.
